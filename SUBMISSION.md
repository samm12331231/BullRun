# BullRun — AI Options Trading Desk with Deterministic Risk Gates

**Category:** Alpaca AI Trading Agents Hackathon ($6,300 Prize Track)  
**Authors:** BullRun Core Architecture Team  
**Stack:** Python 3.11, Next.js 16 (Turbopack, Tailwind CSS v4, Inter Typography), Alpaca Options Trading API, OpenAI GPT-4o-mini, NumPy, Pandas, TA-Lib Indicators, SHA-256 Hash-Chained Audit Engine.

---

### Paragraph 1: The Problem & The Solution
Retail options traders lose billions each year not because they lack market opinions, but because options pricing contains non-linear tail risks—delta decay, volatility collapse, and catastrophic undefined margin losses—that retail platforms fail to explain. Most AI trading agents exacerbate this problem by acting as black-box autonomous bots that hallucinate order sizes or enter naked positions without guardrails. **BullRun** re-architects retail options trading with an institutional multi-agent paradigm: **AI proposes, Evidence decides, Humans authorize.** BullRun restricts all trading to mathematical defined-risk debit spreads (Bull Call Spreads and Bear Put Spreads) where the absolute worst-case loss is capped to the exact cash debit paid at entry.

### Paragraph 2: Multi-Agent Architecture & Deterministic 12-Gate Risk Engine
BullRun orchestrates four specialized agents working in strict separation of concerns. The **Scout Agent** ingests historical bars from Alpaca to mathematically classify market regime (ADX trend strength, EMA 20/50 alignment, MACD momentum, ATR volatility ratios). The **Quant Agent** scans live Alpaca option chains, calculates Black-Scholes Greeks ($\Delta$, $\Gamma$, $\Theta$, $\text{Vega}$, $\text{IV}$), filters for liquid strikes (bid-ask spread $\le \$0.15$), and scores setups with a multi-factor conviction formula. Before any proposal can ever reach the user, our non-negotiable **Deterministic Risk Engine** evaluates 12 mathematical gates: the 2% maximum equity rule, conviction-scaled sizing, a 6% total portfolio heat cap, a 3-concurrent position ceiling, a directional correlation guard across indices, time-of-day market guards (blocking opening and closing 30-minute volatility spikes), and an earnings proximity buffer ($\le 5\text{ DTE}$). The LLM has zero authority over risk gates—it is solely utilized by the **CIO Agent** to translate quantitative structures into transparent, plain-English educational theses.

### Paragraph 3: Real-Time Execution, Dynamic Autopilot, and Verifiable Auditability
Once the human trader reviews the Bloomberg-grade interactive terminal and explicitly authorizes the trade, the **Execution Engine** submits atomic multi-leg limit orders through the Alpaca Trading API with exponential backoff retries and two-leg fill verification. Post-entry, the **Position Monitor** acts as an institutional autopilot, tracking account-native P&L, executing partial profit-taking ($50\%$ of contracts at $+30\%$ profit), enforcing stop-loss proximity alerts, and triggering automated Greeks-based capital preservation exits if long delta drops below $0.10$. Every single signal, proposal, risk verdict, human authorization, and execution fill is cryptographically sealed into an immutable **SHA-256 hash-chained audit log**. BullRun is not just an algorithmic trading system—it is an interactive trading academy that empowers everyday investors to trade options with the discipline, mathematics, and transparency of an institutional options desk.
