# BullRun — Teaching Quantitative Trading to Beginners

BullRun is an AI options trading desk that combines four specialized agents—Scout for market-regime detection, Quant for defined-risk options structure selection, Risk Engine for deterministic safety gates, and CIO for plain-English thesis generation—into one accountable pipeline. A proposal is validated before it reaches the human, then explicit consent is required before the execution layer sends an approved order to Alpaca; after entry, pre-authorized exit rules and monitoring manage the position. The architecture separates quantitative decision logic from language generation so the system can explain a trade without letting an LLM control risk.

What makes BullRun different is that the explanation is part of the product, not a footnote. The teaching engine connects each decision to market context, shows historical outcomes when the journal has enough comparable data, defines options vocabulary in plain English, and progressively exposes complexity from beginner explanations to Greeks and full technical mechanics. The trade card makes max loss tangible with a risk meter, exposes all six conviction factors, explains the wrong-way scenario, and lets users inspect the complete strategy. Most importantly, the risk boundary is deterministic: hard gates are enforced by Python rather than by model judgment, making the safety layer reproducible, auditable, and impossible for a generated thesis to override.

Why it matters: beginners should not have to choose between educational theory and opaque automation. BullRun lets them learn by watching a quantitative workflow happen in front of them—signal, proposal, risk validation, teaching, consent, execution, and post-trade comparison. A rejected trade is a lesson too: the system explains which constraint failed and why waiting can be the disciplined decision. That turns an AI trading agent into an interactive classroom for the reasoning, payoff structure, uncertainty, and risk discipline behind options trading.

This isn't just a trading bot. It's a trading school.
